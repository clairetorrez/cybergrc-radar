GRC Engineering Career Roadmap

Six levels of real-world engineering responsibility. Explore what engineers are expected to know, build, operate, and own, then find resources to develop those skills.

Levels reflect scope, independence, system complexity, and impact, not the number of certifications earned. This is a proposed career framework, not a universal employer grading standard. Training and labs help build capability, but production experience matters.

      Learning resources and projects

      Club credentials and projects are learning resources, not automatic level assignments. External resources are included alongside club offerings.

      How certifications fit

      Introductory club certifications, including CGO as described by members, belong in the foundational learning portion of the roadmap. Completing a certification does not by itself establish professional engineering level. More advanced club certifications and projects can support higher competencies when paired with independently demonstrated work.
